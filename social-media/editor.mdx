The **Social Media Variable Editor** is designed to accelerate the creation and customization of social content. Unlike the Brandbook, here the focus is on **speed and flexibility to generate final assets** across multiple formats.

## What you can do here

- **Onboarding** → Input client and studio information (name, contact, social handles) that applies automatically across templates and mockups.
- **Primitives** → Configure base palettes to differentiate Studio/Designer branding from Client branding.
- **Typography** → Adjust font families, weights, and global text sizes.
- **Spacing Social Media** → Adapt layouts to standard social media formats (e.g. 1080×1080, 1080×1920, 1920×1080).
- **Theming & Mode** → Switch instantly between Light/Dark and apply brand themes.
- **Export** → Download assets optimized for Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook in the correct resolution and proportions.
