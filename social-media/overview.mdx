The **Orbital Supply Social Media System** is designed to streamline the entire content creation pipeline — from internal proposals and client presentations to final exports of ready-to-publish assets.

Built for designers, studios, and digital teams, this system removes the friction of repetitive formatting and manual adjustments. With dynamic variables, responsive templates, and export-ready workflows, you can go from idea to published post in a fraction of the time.

## Who it’s for

- **Studios & agencies** → deliver fast, professional proposals and consistent client assets.
- **Freelancers & designers** → save hours by using variable-driven templates and auto-layout systems.
- **Marketing teams** → Ensure content is always on-brand, optimized for each platform, and ready to export in seconds.

## Why it’s different

- ⚡ **Client & Studio branding**: Use dual branding modes to separate proposals from final deliverables.
- 🎨 **Dynamic variables**: Change colors, numbers, and strings (e.g. client name, social handles) across the entire file instantly.
- 🧩 **14 customizable carousel templates**: Built for diverse content types — educational, case studies, job offers, inspiration, company updates, and more.
- 📐 **Responsive auto-layout system**: Posts adapt seamlessly to different platform formats.
- 📤 **Export Station**: One-click export to correct formats and resolutions for Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook.
- 🔀 **Flexible slots**: Swap components inside presentations without breaking layouts.
- ⚙️ **Nested component properties**: Edit cards, tables, pages, or entire mockups in a single click.

> The Social Media System isn’t just a template — it’s a full workflow. From proposals to final assets, every step is modular, consistent, and built to save time while amplifying creativity.
