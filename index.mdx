## Why Orbital Supply?

Most templates are just pretty pictures. Ours are **systems**.

- **Variable-First**: Every color, font, and space is controlled by global tokens.
- **Production-Grade**: Naming conventions match real code (Tailwind, Shadcn).
- **Editor-Powered**: Sync your Figma files with our external Variable Editor for instant customization.
- **Multi-Brand**: Built-in support for Light/Dark modes and Client/Studio themes.
