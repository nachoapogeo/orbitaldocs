The **Orbital Supply Brandbook** is more than a template — it’s a modular system designed to bring clarity, consistency, and scalability to your brand identity. Built for designers, studios, and digital teams who value both precision and autonomy, it transforms the traditional brand guidelines into a flexible, living document.

Where most brandbooks feel rigid and generic, ours adapts to your process. With pre-built structures, editable variables, and smart theming, you can create a professional brand guide in minutes — not weeks. Every section is designed to be intuitive, ensuring you spend less time formatting and more time designing.

## Who it’s for

- **Designers & studios** → need a fast, professional way to deliver brand guidelines.
- **Startups & teams** → want a scalable system for consistency across touchpoints.
- **Creators & makers** → seek autonomy, clarity, and a frictionless workflow.

## Why it’s different

- ⚡ **Modular architecture**: Add, remove, or rearrange sections effortlessly.
- 🎨 **Editable in minutes**: Update colors, typography, and structure without breaking consistency.
- 🚀 **Scalable output**: Works for solo designers or growing organizations.

It’s a system that grows with you — giving you structure without rigidity, clarity without complexity, and freedom without losing control.

> Inside, you’ll find everything from variable-based color systems to ready-made components for typography, layout, and brand voice — all designed to work seamlessly together.
